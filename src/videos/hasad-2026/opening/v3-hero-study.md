# v3 · Hero study (the Saudi engineer)

## Who he is
- An engineer at the Saudi Irrigation Organization (المؤسسة العامة للري), late thirties, with a **short neat black beard**, calm intelligent features, slim build.
- **The approved face:** M-02A (the night scene); it is the reference for every drawing.
- In the story he is **alone**. He works hard (stays up late, fails, has the idea), and at the end stands satisfied at the canal.

## Saudi thobe (distinguishing it from the Emirati one)
- **Collar:** the Saudi thobe is told apart by its **high, stiff band collar with two buttons**. The Emirati kandura is collarless with a tassel. **This detail matters so the character doesn't read as Emirati.**
- **Sleeves:** shirt-style cuffs with **visible cufflinks**.
- **Cut:** a slim fit (tighter than its Gulf neighbours), ankle length.
- **Colour:** white (summer). In winter, dark heavy fabrics (navy and brown) are an option for later scenes.
- **Engineer details (my addition, kept within reason):** a **pen in the chest pocket**, a **leather-strap wristwatch**, and black leather **sandals (na'al)** in the office.

## Shemagh and agal
- **The Saudi shemagh:** **red-and-white** checks. The colour is **deep crimson on white**, not pink; the first test was pink → a correction.
- Folded into a triangle with a sharp **peak at the front** above the forehead. Its sides fall onto the shoulders or are tossed back.
- **Taqiyah:** a white cotton/crocheted cap underneath. It barely shows at the edge of the forehead.
- **Agal:** a black cord worn **doubled** (two rings) on the crown, about two fingers above the eyebrows, with **two cord tails hanging at the back**.

## Anime proportions
- An adult man in realistic anime: **7–8 heads** tall. I chose **7.5** so he is elegant and realistic, not childlike.
- The face is built from simple shapes: a clear jaw and short beard, eyes on the head's midline, and details reduced to the essential lines.
- Clean outlines, flat colour in two or three tones, a warm rim of light. This matches the cel style of the environments.

## Expressions the story needs
1. Calm focus (the start, sunset)
2. Deep concentration while drawing (lips pressed)
3. Tired (rubbing his eyes, at night)
4. Frustrated (crumpling the paper)
5. The idea (eyes wide, a small smile, at dawn)
6. Contentment (eyes closed, a calm smile, a deep breath, at the canal)
